# Mission brief

A flood-response analyst needs information about possible road-access problems in a **1.60 km² area**. A UAV mapping mission has been proposed. Your team must recommend whether to proceed with stated conditions, revise the mission, use another source of information, delay, or not fly.

A useful result must address the analyst's question. An image of water alone does not prove a road is safe or unsafe to drive on. Distinguish visible observations from conclusions that require other evidence.

## Proposed mission conditions

These are stipulated planning inputs for the fictional mission, not conclusions from the experimental datasets:

| Input | Planning value |
|---|---|
| Required area | 1.60 km² |
| Payload | 600 g |
| Nominal wind speed | 3 m/s |
| Planned ground speed | 8 m/s |
| Effective image swath | 200 m |
| Nonproductive time | 10 minutes |
| Usable battery energy | 300 Wh, with the required reserve already excluded |

Do not subtract the reserve a second time. Select tests that apply to these conditions, and state any mismatch. Mean electrical power and coverage efficiency are not supplied as established values in this starter; investigate them when the relevant package is released. If a required quantity remains unknown, retain it as an unknown in your calculation.

## Your responsibilities
- Identify the test conditions before using a measurement.
- Select relevant evidence and explain why less comparable tests are not used, or are used only with qualifications.
- Check units, missing values and the meaning of each column.
- Separate measured or simulated records, assumptions, calculations and interpretations.
- State what the available evidence establishes and what remains unknown.
- Propose the next test or information that would most improve the decision.

## Evidence release
You will see five package titles but not their contents before the vote. The instructor will publish the selected package after the final vote. All earlier released packages remain available. Your calculation must use the information available on your class's actual path. If you lack an input, show a conditional relationship or bound and name the missing input.

## Safety and scope
This is a fictional planning exercise with simulated data. There will be no flight activity. Missing control or exposure evidence cannot be treated as permission to fly. A class vote selects what to investigate, not whether safety requirements apply.

## Final recommendation
Use this structure: “We recommend ___ under these conditions: ___. Our strongest evidence is ___ in file ___, rows/test IDs ___. We cannot yet establish ___. The next useful check is ___ because ___.”
