# UAV mission investigation
## 2145211 Introduction to Aerospace Engineering

Class repository: https://github.com/vtaerodoctor-hokie/2145211-2026-uav-investigation

You are advising a flood-response team. Decide whether a proposed UAV mission can produce useful information and whether the available evidence supports that mission. Use AI to help inspect data, but make and explain your own engineering decisions.

**All case data are simulated for teaching. This exercise does not authorize a real flight.**

## Before class
1. Complete [the setup guide](setup/BEFORE-CLASS.md).
2. Read [the mission brief](MISSION.md) and [the concept notebook](CONCEPT-NOTEBOOK.md).
3. Keep your own fork. You will receive evidence by syncing it after the class vote.

## During class
The class selects three of five packages. Each cycle includes individual choice, group discussion, an initial group vote, minority defense, a final group vote, information release, teaching, AI practice, an independent paper task, upload and reflection.

| Package | Main question | Tutor prompt |
|---|---|---|
| ENERGY | Which tests support our energy and endurance estimate? | [Energy](prompts/ENERGY.md) |
| COVERAGE | How much useful area can the mission cover? | [Coverage](prompts/COVERAGE.md) |
| CONTROL | What do the tests establish about loss of control inputs? | [Control](prompts/CONTROL.md) |
| DATA | Will the observations answer the customer's question? | [Information quality](prompts/DATA.md) |
| EXPOSURE | Who could be affected, and what remains unverified? | [Exposure](prompts/EXPOSURE.md) |

Do not edit the instructor's files in `evidence/`. Keep your own non-sensitive working notes in `work/`. Only use packages the instructor has released. A missing package is not evidence that its requirements have passed.

## What to hand in
- Complete the individual paper task without AI during each cycle. Submit the requested photograph through the instructor's displayed private submission link.
- Use the group decision sheet to record initial and final votes, reasons, and any change of mind.
- Maintain a short [decision log](work/decision-log.md) in your fork. Do not publish your student ID, grades, assessment answers, identifiable photographs or classmates' information.

## When a tool fails
Use the instructor's released download or view a partner's copy. Continue the paper reasoning. Tell the instructor what failed; do not spend the independent-task period troubleshooting software.
